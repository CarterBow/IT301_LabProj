Getting Started

During your time at James Madison University, you will experience so many wonderful, challenging, and redefining moments that you might not know how to navigate.

This survival guide will be your secret weapon to winning at college, and your fail safe when you get stuck in a challenge.

In this guide, you can expect to find information on a wide range of topics, including academics, career, campus, and recreation resources. Each section will provide you with tips, advice, and resources to help you succeed in your college journey.
