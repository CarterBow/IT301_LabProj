PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Start Building Your Career Early

Career preparation does not have to wait until senior year. Students should begin building experience, making connections, and exploring career options all throughout college.

Career Center Resources

Resume Help

Career advisors help students improve their resumes and better describe their education, skills, work experience, and projects. You can find them in the SSC, or through connections in your major. Get to know some people!

Career Advising

Students can meet with career professionals to discuss internships, career paths, job searches, and professional development.

Career Closet and Headshots

Professional clothing and headshot opportunities can help students prepare for interviews, career fairs, and LinkedIn profiles. At the Career Closet, students can take home up to 5 pieces of clothing per semester... For FREE!

Opportunities

Handshake

Handshake allows students to search for jobs and internships, learn about employers, and find career events available to JMU students.

Career Fairs

Career fairs give students the opportunity to speak directly with employers, learn about open positions, and practice professional networking. This is one of my favorite ways to build professional experience and connections.

Experience

Build Experience

Internships, research, campus jobs, volunteer work, and student organizations all provide valuable experience before graduation. Make connections with professors, advisors, and peers to find opportunities that fit your interests.
