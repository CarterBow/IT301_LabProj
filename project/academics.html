THE STUDENT RESOURCE BOARD

Academic Success at JMU

College classes require more independence and organization. JMU offers several resources that help students stay on track and get support when and where they need it.

RESOURCE 01

Canvas and MyMadison

Canvas

Canvas is where most professors post assignments, grades, announcements, course materials, and important deadlines.

MyMadison

MyMadison is mainly used for tasks such as viewing your schedule, registering for classes, and accessing university information. However, MyMadison is also where you will find information about billing, scholarships, employment info, and just about any other university notification.

RESOURCE 02

Academic Support

Libraries

JMU libraries provide study spaces, research materials, rentable technology, and help with finding reliable academic sources.

Tutoring

Tutoring and academic support services can help students better understand difficult material and prepare for exams. Tutoring can be found in the SSC or in the SMLC.

Notes to Remember

Keep track of assignments in a calendar, review material throughout the week, start large projects early, and find a study environment that works well for you.
