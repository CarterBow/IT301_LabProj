LIFE OUTSIDE THE CLASSROOM

Recreation at JMU

Recreation is a great way to stay active, reduce stress, meet new people, and take a break from academics. At JMU, the options for recreation are everywhere. Find one that works for you!

01 / FITNESS

UREC

University Recreation provides fitness spaces, courts, activity areas, classes, and other recreational opportunities for students.

Fitness Areas

Students can use strength equipment, cardio machines, and other workout areas for individual exercise.

Courts and Activity Spaces

UREC also includes spaces for activities such as basketball, racquet sports, and other recreational games.

02 / SPORTS

Intramural and Club Sports

Intramural Sports

Intramural sports allow students to form teams and participate in casual competition with other JMU students.

Club Sports

Club sports provide a more organized and competitive experience for students interested in specific sports.

03 / OUTDOORS

Outdoor Activities

Students can also explore outdoor recreation opportunities, equipment rentals, and activities away from traditional gym spaces. Equipment is available for rent through UREC.

New to the Gym?

Don't be afraid to start! UREC offers personal training, tutorials, and a community of Dukes who are willing to help. UREC is a judgment-free zone.
