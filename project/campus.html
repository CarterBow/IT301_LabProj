Life on Campus

Campus life is more than attending classes. Learning how to get around, where to eat, and how to get involved can make JMU feel like home.

Getting Around

Students can travel around campus by walking, biking, driving, or using the ICS (the campus bus route). Learning your path between classes early will make your semesters much smoother. A personal favorite is biking. Its fast, environmentally friendly, and a great source of exercise.

Dining

JMU offers several dining locations across campus, ranging from dining halls to coffee shops. While on campus, you've got to eat!

Dining Halls

Ehall and Dhall dining halls provide all you can eat food, with a rotating menus daily. Alongside that there are lots of restaraunts like Chick-fil-a, Panda Express, and more. Students can use their meal plan or pay with cash/card.

Student Organizations

Student organizations are one of the easiest ways to meet people with similar interests, explore new activities, and become involved in the JMU community. Keep an eye out for Student Org night at the beginning of the semesters.

Campus Events

Throughout the semester, students can attend athletic events, performances, club meetings, university programs, and other activities happening around campus. Most of the time, these events are free for students and they may even provide you with food or free items.
